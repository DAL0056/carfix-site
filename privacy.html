CarFix privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

What the app uses

When you sign in with Google, CarFix asks for your email address and access to a hidden app-data folder in your own Google Drive. CarFix can only see the files it creates in that folder. It cannot see any of your other Drive files.

What is stored

Your garage (vehicles), repair journal, photos, manuals and settings are stored on your device and, if you turn on sync, in that hidden folder in your own Google Drive, so your devices stay in step.

What is shared

Nothing is sent to the developer. CarFix has no server and no analytics. When you ask the AI helper (ALEX) a question, your question and the relevant car data are sent to the AI provider you choose in settings (for example Google Gemini), using your own API key.

Removing your data

Sign out in CarFix to stop syncing. You can delete the app data at any time in Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → CarFix → Delete hidden app data, and remove CarFix's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
